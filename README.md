## Overview

PlayMore TV is an all-in-one media center designed specifically for Smart TVs, Fire TV, and Android TV streaming devices. Browse movies and TV shows (TMDB), stream live IPTV channels with full TV program guides (EPG), integrate third-party add-ons, auto-skip anime intros, check live sports scores, and sync your watch progress across devices.

> **Disclaimer**: PlayMore TV is a media player application. It does not provide, host, or distribute any media content, streams, or playlists. Users must supply their own media sources, IPTV playlists, or add-ons.

---

## Features

- **Built for TV Remotes**: Smooth, easy D-pad navigation designed specifically for your television.
- **Movies & TV Shows**: Rich media cataloging, trailers, cast info, and recommendations powered by TMDB.
- **Live TV & EPG Guide**: Full IPTV playlist support (M3U, Xtream) with interactive TV program guides (XMLTV).
- **Add-on Support**: Connect your favorite Stremio-compatible add-ons for custom media catalogs and streams.
- **Multiple Profiles**: Separate accounts for family members with independent watch history and favorites.
- **Cloud Sync**: Keep your profiles, playlists, and watch history in sync across all your TVs.
- **Anime Auto-Skip**: Automatic "Skip Intro" and "Skip Outro" buttons for anime series.
- **Live Sports Scores**: Real-time sports scoreboards and upcoming game schedules.

## How to Install

### Supported Devices
Works on any Android TV, Google TV, or Amazon Fire TV device running Android 7.0 or higher.

### Installation Steps

1. **Download the App**:
   - Get the latest `.apk` file from the Releases Page.

2. **Install on Your TV**:
   - **Using the Downloader App** (Recommended):
     1. Open the Downloader app on your TV (available on Fire TV Appstore / Google Play).
     2. Enter the direct download link to the APK.
     3. Click Install when prompted.
   - **Using Send Files to TV**:
     1. Download the APK onto your phone or computer.
     2. Use the Send Files to TV app to transfer it to your TV.
     3. Open a file manager on your TV and install the APK.

## Privacy

PlayMore TV is local-first:
- Your settings, profiles, and viewing history stay on your device or in your private cloud sync.
- No personal viewing data or analytics are tracked or collected.
